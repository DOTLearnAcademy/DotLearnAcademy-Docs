# ADR-001 — Payment Gateway Selection

**Date:** 03/04/2026  
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Razorpay

## Reason
- Audience is primarily Indian learners
- Supports UPI, NetBanking, Wallets, Cards, EMI
- T+2 INR settlement speed
- SAQ A PCI compliance via Razorpay checkout.js
- No international currency needed for v1
